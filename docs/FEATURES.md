# Feature reference

A complete description of every HyperQuant screen and panel. For an overview, start with the [README](../README.md); for the maths behind the numbers, see [Methods and literature](METHODS.md).

The interface is available in English and Polish. For readers of the Polish version, the Polish UI label is given in *italics* next to the English name.

## Today (*Dziś*, `#dzis`; formerly Start, old `#start` links still open it)

The three phases of the day with a 24 h session dial, **instrument cards** for the desk markets (table above) with the LIVE price and reading time, funding and open interest (a click opens In session with that market's chart), then the BTC Four numbers and short chapters leading to the phase screens.

## Pre-session (*Przed sesją*, `#before`)

The chart with the preparation layouts (Day map: 30m with the TPO day profile, VWAP, PDH/PDL and the range cone; Fuel and hours), then **Four numbers** (*Czwórka*: yesterday's legibility, the liquidation fuel within ±1% of price, the typical width of the session's next hour, distance from VWAP; a description, not a signal, with a **Frame** button), a context strip (2 h range from Monte Carlo, book depth, VPIN/λ, OU, distance from the session VWAP in σ, Initial Balance state, funding, the next macro release), the Fed/BLS/BEA calendar with countdown, funding on Hyperliquid vs Binance vs Bybit (normalised to APR) with a crowding z-score, **auction context** for the current London or New York session (VWAP ±1σ/±2σ, Initial Balance of the session's first hour with extensions, the previous day's profile, weak highs and lows, next to historical frequencies from ~200 BTC sessions with Wilson intervals), the liquidation map with cascade, **book liquidity by UTC hour** with the execution cost of your size, macro tiles (UST yields, TGA, RRP, CFTC COT, Fear & Greed, mempool) and RSS headlines. The context strip shows a connecting state until the first API sync.

## In session (*W sesji*, `#charts`)

The desk markets (table above) on the TradingView Lightweight Charts™ engine with live Hyperliquid data (each market with its own tape, price tick and book aggregation); pick the market in the chart bar, switch between them with `M`, or open either chart from the ⌘K / Ctrl+K command palette. **Layouts** (*Układy*), picked from the **Layout ▾** list that opens the single chart bar (layout, market, timeframe, chart type, **Layers ▾** with each layer's source badge and data age, Indicators, then the layout's numbers, *State*, the DOM column and a **⋯** menu with drawing, tape, alerts, settings, 1/2/4 charts, scale, replay, link, screenshot, Frame, shortcuts and full screen), grouped by phase of the day: Day map and Fuel and hours (Pre-session), Cockpit, Auction and open, and Big hands and the rest of the market (In session), Day review (Post-session). Each sets the chart layout, timeframes, layers and panels for one trader question in a single click, the layout's question is the tooltip of its name, and three numbers in the bar with a LIVE or MODEL badge (e.g. distance from VWAP in σ, **range budget**, **RVOL**; no data is "—" with a reason; a tap shows the source); Alt+1…6 switches between them (and moves to the matching phase screen), your changes are kept as your own version of the layout, **blog map** levels (the author's map, BTC only, with the post's time) can be drawn on the chart, the **Frame** (*Klatka*) button saves a 1200×675 or 1080×1080 PNG with a title, time, source and an always-present data-quality strip (tape and map coverage), and on the first visit a short *How to read this screen* card with a replay of the last 15 minutes for 7 days, and a reset button returns to the template. Your own **tabs** are kept: old workstation links and tabs map onto the new layouts, and the *Restore my previous settings* bar brings back the former Start tab (the old tab list is backed up). On phones the header is one row (logo, price, change; the other market numbers, language and stream pause are under the price and in *More*), and one horizontally scrolling row holds the layout, market, timeframe and chart-type lists and the **Chart, Tape, DOM and Numbers** cards (shown one at a time; the five flow numbers Book, Tape, Who, Walls, TWAP are in Numbers), with Layers and a ⋯ menu pinned at its end; the price chart takes at least 60% of the screen (one panel under it, volume at the bottom of the price chart, a one-line legend with the layout's three numbers, whale labels on tap). A tap on an empty part of the chart switches **focus mode** (only the chart and the bottom menu, the price chart ≥ 80% of the screen), and a phone turned sideways shows the chart full screen. The Cockpit starts on a phone with candles, VWAP and the heatmap; bubbles, walls and large orders are one tap away in Layers. Timeframes 1m–1D with history from the API (loaded further as you scroll left), local time.

## In session — tape, footprint and replay

The **last 24 hours of tape and order book** come from the scanner's recorder in the public data repository, and the tape is also cached in the browser, so footprint, Delta, CVD, bubbles and the heatmap are there from the first second. **Footprint** from the trade tape: zoomed out a plain candle, closer a volume profile inside the candle, closer still cells with numbers on every price row. Styles Bid × Ask (aggressor sells × buys; on wide candles also row delta and volume columns), Delta and Volume; automatic or fixed row height, framed POC, 70% value area, diagonal imbalances (adjustable ratio and minimum USD value) and stacked zones. **Delta** panel (delta candles: max and min delta during the bar) and **CVD** panel (reset per Asia/London/New York session with DST, UTC day, week, or continuous), volume from the API, and optional CVD and ΔOI panels comparing Hyperliquid with Binance and Bybit; every panel under the chart, including the Numbers table, is picked in the Indicators menu (sections Favourites, On the chart, Order flow, Classic as one list, Below the chart and HyperQuant only, with search; OBV, CMF, MFI and Net Volume were removed) and can be hidden with the cross in its corner. **Market state** (*Stan rynku*): one sentence (folded to the *State* button, one line under the bar when opened) built from fixed rules on the last 10 candles (price vs daily VWAP in σ, CVD direction and how long it has lasted, whether Binance and Bybit agree, open-interest change, the side of large players and the rest, book imbalance), with a "why?" panel showing the numbers behind each part; a description, not a signal. **Replay** (*Powtórka*) of the recorded tape and order book: up to the last 24 hours played back on the same chart at 1×, 5×, 15× or 60× with a time slider, as if it were live (footprint, heatmap, bubbles, CVD, Who is trading, Market state); a link opens the replay at a given moment, optionally with a layout, layers and a highlighted price zone.

## In session — indicators, drawing, whales and profiles

**Indicators** (*Wskaźniki*) from candle history, visible immediately: daily VWAP with ±1σ/±2σ bands and session VWAP, visible-range volume profile with POC/VAH/VAL, PDH/PDL and daily open, session opens, initial balance with 1.5× and 2× extensions, liquidation clusters from the scanner (BTC, and any other main-exchange market whose map passes the coverage and freshness bar: the layer's description shows the coverage, or why the market has no map yet), each toggled in the Indicators menu. **Drawing tools** like TradingView (left toolbar): lines, channel, Fibonacci, shapes, measure with tape volume and delta, long/short position with R:R, text and notes, fixed-range volume profile, anchored VWAP; magnet, undo, keyboard shortcuts (list under "?"), saved in the browser. **Address-level clashes**: aggressor order bubbles above a market-adjusted threshold, battles with one large maker, bursts of small orders from many addresses, maker walls and icebergs (a maker absorbed more than the level ever showed in the book), and **large resting orders in the book** (average order size of a level above a threshold, drawn as a line for as long as it rests); a click shows details and the taker's account pseudonym (never the address) with a summary of its orders, and the **order tape** (Time & Sales) has a value filter and a sound for large orders. **Whales**: positions and large resting orders (including stops and take-profits) of the largest Hyperliquid accounts, under a pseudonym valid for one UTC day. **Day profile** (Market Profile, timeframes up to 30m): a volume profile of every UTC day coloured by the aggressive side from the tape (the last 7 days from day profiles the recorder publishes after each UTC day closes), with a thin TPO time outline (how many 30-minute periods touched each price), POC and value area from volume, first-hour range, single prints, tails, naked POCs, the day delta and row numbers under the crosshair; optionally coloured by **positions** instead: the minute open-interest change assigned to prices by volume, a solid bar where OI rose (new longs or new shorts by the side leading the minute) and an outline where it fell (shorts or longs closing). **Numbers table** (*Tablica liczb*) under the chart, in groups: Hyperliquid (volume, delta, ΔOI, 8 h funding), tape (delta %, delta extremes, trades, pace), **Who is trading** (*Kto handluje*: delta of large players and of the rest of the market from taker accounts over the 60 minutes up to the candle close, and the large players' share of entry value; sums only, never accounts) and optionally Binance (perp and spot delta, ΔOI, funding) and Bybit (delta, ΔOI); also **imbalance zones** and **unfinished auctions** extended to the right until price closes them. A one-line legend with OHLC and (with the VWAP layer) VWAP and the distance from it in σ, the row under the cursor; delta, its extremes, trade count and tape share of bar volume in the Delta or CVD pane caption; value labels for at most the 5 largest bubbles, walls and large orders in view (3 on a phone, the rest on hover; *Label density* 0, 3, 5 or all in Chart settings), never over each other, level tags or the last 10 candles; the live DOM ladder in its own margin next to the price axis; grid labels on the price axis give way to the last price, its countdown and VWAP; PNG screenshot with caption, full screen.

## In session — alerts and multi-chart layouts

**Price alerts** from the bell panel, the right-click menu or a horizontal line: touch, candle close (1 to 3 in a row) or reclaim of a level on 1m to 4h candles, with an optional delta filter, active hours, OCO pairs and chains, plus a **squeeze** alert with no level (a move of a set % within 10 minutes while open interest falls at least 0.3%, i.e. positions being closed rather than opened); alert lines can be hidden on all charts with one switch while the alerts keep running; each alert reports the candle's delta, volume, OI change and largest orders (sound, browser notification and history while the page is open in any tab; with **24/7 notifications** on, the HyperQuant server watches up to 30 alerts per device and sends a Web Push even with the page closed and the phone locked, no account needed; a ready set loads from a link after confirmation), a **view link** with market, timeframe and chart type, a chart menu with horizontal line, copy price and screenshot. On wide screens, **layouts of 1, 2 or 4 charts** (each with its own market, timeframe and settings; shared drawings per market, alerts and crosshair) and your own **setting templates**, saved from the current settings. The layouts of this phase are Cockpit (default), Auction and open, and Big hands and the rest of the market.

## In session — DOM & tape column

From 1440 px a **DOM & tape column** stands next to the chart (key `d` hides it); from 640 to 1439 px the same column is a drawer over the chart, closed at the start (the header button or `d` opens it, Esc closes it); on phones its five numbers are in the chart's Numbers card. The ladder and the tape follow the chart's market (BTC or gold); below the chart sit BTC cohort flow and the realised BTC liquidation tape. Scanner panels (Who absorbs the hits, TWAPs en route, cohorts, liquidations) cover BTC only: on gold they show "—, no scanner" with the reason; the blog map and HIP-4 layers show "—, BTC only". The column's panels: **Who is in the queue**: a ladder on a fixed price grid every 1, 10 or 100 USD with size, order count and average order per level (one large hand or a crowd), a 15-minute heat strip at each price, the value of trades that hit the level, and whale orders under a daily pseudonym, including stops and take-profits that are not in the book. **Walls: eaten or pulled**: when a large level disappears, a verdict from comparing ~5 s book snapshots (eaten by trades at its price, moved to nearby levels, or pulled), with counts for the last hour, how far from the price walls vanish (at the price, 1, 2–4, 5+ levels) and verdict icons where a wall's trace ends on the heat strip; a net comparison, not spoofing detection. **Tape with memory**: aggressor orders with one account's TWAP slices glued into one growing row, battles with one maker, repeat accounts (session pseudonym) and tape pace against a normal minute. **TWAPs en route**: TWAPs executing right now, matched to the account's plan from the public Hyperliquid TWAP log, with progress, what is left, the next 15 minutes, buy/sell totals and the TWAP share of the tape. **Who absorbs the hits**: Hyperliquid names the maker of every trade, so the view shows how many accounts absorb selling (bids) and buying (asks) over 15 minutes and how much the top three take, next to the same for aggressors and the usual share of the last hour (pseudonyms only). **Head of the queue**: best bid and ask from the `bbo` channel several times a second, how long the price has stood, and how much of the head's depletion was eaten by trades versus cancelled. A strip of five live numbers sits on top (one per panel); a click on a number enlarges its panel within the column. The last hour comes from the scanner's recording.

## Post-session (*Po sesji*, `#after`)

The chart with the Day review layout, then the **forecast ledger** (the verifiable desk ledger: scores, calibration, one-click verification in the browser, the cat model's record, the A/B plan of model versions) and the **session review** (*Rozliczenie*, anchor `#rozliczenie`: cards for the last closed London and New York sessions and the one in progress with day type (Dalton), value-area migration, Initial Balance result, range against the expected one judged ex ante, hit and missed blog map levels and liquidations; below them the 30-day forecast cone and 24 h cohorts, with replay links) and the **trade journal and playbook**: setups in a context → location → trigger → execution → invalidation template, trades with an R result from the initial stop, MAE/MFE (in R) and the auction context at entry reconstructed from 1m Hyperliquid candles, per-setup statistics with uncertainty (hit rate with a Wilson interval, mean R with a bootstrap interval, profit factor, median MAE of winners and MFE of losers); data stays in the browser, JSON export/import.

## Markets (*Rynki*, `#markets`; in the top bar, on phones under *More*)

One instrument card per desk market (table above), each with the exchange and market class, the price with the market's tick and a LIVE badge with the reading time, 24 h volume, open interest in USD (contracts × mark price), hourly funding with the annualised rate (× 24 × 365) and a **Chart** button that opens the market in session. Your browser asks Hyperliquid directly, one `metaAndAssetCtxs` per exchange every 60 s and only while the tab is visible (weight 20 each, with this page's weight in the last minute shown against the per-IP limit); the HyperQuant server fetches nothing. A reading older than 3 minutes or a market missing from the exchange response is "—" with the reason. No price change and no judgement of price moves.

## Lessons (*Lekcje*, under *More* and in the footer)

Six "What would you do?" exercises on real BTC days (20–24 Oct 2026): the chart stops at a key moment showing only closed candles (the price axis too), you answer or mark the invalidation level on the chart, the reasoning is scored (2/1/0) with feedback for every answer, then the rest of the day is revealed candle by candle, followed by a debrief with an honest caveat, the related Folklore lab result and a button that opens the matching In session layout. Also an atlas of the In session indicators (what it is, how we compute it, how to read it, traps, what to combine it with), how to combine them, popular rules next to the Folklore lab data, and a risk lab (1000 simulated trade series with fixed-fractional risk and costs, labelled as a simulation). VWAP, profile, IB and footprint use the same functions as the In session chart; slices are built by `scripts/lessons/build.mjs` from the public data repository. In Polish and English (times in UTC in the English version). No signals, no promised results. **Folklore lab**: 10 BTC day-trading rules (round numbers, PDH/PDL sweeps, IB breakout, VWAP reversion, CME gap, extreme funding, the "10:00 New York dump", intraday momentum) with a positive control and a placebo: a historical test with FDR correction and a pre-registered forward test (catalogue recorded in the verifiable ledger, e-values valid at every peek, e-BH).

## Research (*Badania*, under *More*)

Tools outside the flow of the day. **Model vs market (HIP-4)**: Hyperliquid outcome markets on BTC ("price above K at expiry", "touch K before expiry", the daily market settling at 06:00 UTC), with the market price from the "Yes" side of the book next to the desk model's probability for the same question (Monte Carlo on seasonal log-HAR + FHS in a Web Worker, exact Brownian-bridge extremes), a liquidity filter and the difference in pp; a calibration comparison, not a signal. Macro proxies from HIP-3 markets (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, gold, oil, EUR, JPY) and the macro share of BTC's move (β, R²); the Hyperliquid perp market map (treemap, OI/volume, 24 h change). Order-book mechanics from the literature (Cont–Kukanov–Stoikov OFI, Hawkes MLE, Stoikov microprice, orders added and cancelled near the price, absorption from regression); liquidity profile and attraction field U(p), regime, heuristic trajectory, OU, Hawkes intensity, Hurst, entropy, VPIN, Kyle's λ, a fixed-risk position-size calculator and the order-flow tape.

## Blog

BTC analyses written before the fact (in Polish): the day map, the map before the New York open and the daily summary, with short notes in between. Every post has a level map with distance from price, scenarios with probabilities, its own link-preview image and share buttons; full analyses also have a data panel, a liquidation map, flows, conditional scenarios and charts (session map with VWAP, profile, delta, CVD and OI; footprint of the key area), and short notes a 6-hour chart. Each post links to the replay of the recorded tape in session at the moment it was published (kept for 7 days). Static pages (SEO), RSS, monthly archive. Posts are Markdown files in `content/blog/`; format and safeguards in [docs/BLOG.md](docs/BLOG.md).

## Sources (*Źródła*, under *More* and in the footer)

Lineage of every number (source → method → LIVE/MODEL → refresh), attributions and licences, the opt-in CEX switch with connection status, tape sound, the Aggr template, and a download of the data of the former Plan Analyzer (plans and equity saved in this browser, exported as a JSON file; the card appears only when such data exists, or when the saved plans cannot be read, which can then be downloaded raw).

## Retired views

The Lab 3D view, the Plan Analyzer and the separate Desk, Market, Microstructure and DOM & tape tabs no longer exist as tabs; their content moved into the phase screens and Research. Old links (`#desk`, `#plan`, `#market`, `#lab`, `#micro`, `#flow`) redirect to the matching screen.
